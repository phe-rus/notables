# Notables privacy information

The official privacy policy is https://pherus.org/legal/privacy-policy.
Section 4 covers Notables. Data deletion instructions are at
https://pherus.org/legal/data-deletion.

The authoritative legal content is maintained in ~/pherus/pherus/www/data/legal
as localized documents. Update that website and the store disclosures together.
This repository does not host a separate legal policy. The official product
showcase is https://pherus.org/showcase/notables.
