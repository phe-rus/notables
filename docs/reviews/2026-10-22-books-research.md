# Books research, 22 October 2026

Primary-source desk research for the Books redesign. Apple URLs without a pinned version currently resolve to the iOS 27 guide. This records documentation, not a physical inspection of Apple Books. No source below specifies the exact opening/closing animation duration, easing, automatic control-hide delay, or a cover morph implementation.

## Documented Apple Books behavior

- **Entry and dismissal:** tapping a cover opens its book. Tapping the page exposes controls; Close dismisses it. Swiping down from the page top also closes the book unless vertical scrolling is enabled. Contents opens from the reading menu; holding Contents and dragging horizontally jumps through the book. Search Book accepts text or a page number. Previous/current location arrows support returning after a jump. The iPhone menu offers portrait/landscape orientation lock. [Apple, Read books on iPhone](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/read-books-iphc1af7c57/ios).
- **Appearance:** Themes & Settings exposes size, font, bold, brightness, background, themes, and Curl/Fast Fade/Scroll. Layout customization includes line, character and word spacing, margins and justification. iPad also documents text-column customization. PDF vertical scrolling has its own appearance control. [Apple, Read books on iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/guide/ipad/ipadc8494b6b/27/ipados/27).
- **Focus:** Line Guide keeps the current line visible and dims the surrounding page. It can move by dragging or tapping above/below; its menu adjusts dimming or disables it. [Apple, Read books on iPhone](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/read-books-iphc1af7c57/ios).
- **Return and bookmarks:** closing saves the reading position automatically. Bookmarks are separate, explicit return points, available through Bookmarks & Highlights. Home surfaces current reading and Library includes imported books, PDFs, samples and audiobooks. [Apple, Read books on iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/guide/ipad/ipadc8494b6b/27/ipados/27).
- **Annotations:** long-press text and adjust selection handles to highlight, underline or add a note. The Highlights list navigates to annotations. Selection actions include search, translation, copying and sharing; highlights/notes can also be shared in batches. [Apple, Annotate books on iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-eg/guide/iphone/iph17bf340c1/ios).
- **Sharing:** selected text uses the system sharing destinations; a Book Store selection can include the store link. The reading menu also shares a book's store link. This documentation does not promise export of an entire protected book. [Apple, Read books on iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/guide/ipad/ipadc8494b6b/27/ipados/27).
- **Playback continuity:** closing the full audiobook player switches to a mini-player; tapping it restores full screen. Stopping/closing that player is a separate action. Playback supports seek, chapter selection, speed, sleep timer, external controls and playback destinations. Supplemental PDFs can be viewed while audio plays. [Apple, Listen to audiobooks on iPhone](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/guide/iphone/iphac1971248/ios).

## Design inferences for Notables

These are proposed interpretations, not claims about Apple's implementation:

- Give the book the entire reading surface. Toggle quiet controls from an unselected page tap, while preserving text selection and links. Keep an explicit accessible Close action alongside gesture dismissal.
- Use the tapped cover as a visual source for opening and returning. Choose and validate Notables' own motion, including Reduce Motion; do not describe guessed timing as an Apple specification.
- Group Contents, search, annotations, appearance and reading aids in the reader menu. Offer direct page/location entry in addition to chapter navigation.
- Keep the stable reading locator separate from bookmarks. Derive Continue and progress from the saved locator. A percentage presentation and its exact Apple placement were not established by the reviewed documentation.
- Keep playback owned above the reader so leaving the book does not destroy audio. The documented mini-player is evidence for continuity within Books; background OS audio behavior still requires platform implementation and device testing.

## Documented Internet Archive and Open Library behavior

- **Catalog is not content availability.** Open Library has records with no readable digital edition. Its FAQ distinguishes Read, Borrow and restricted Preview Only, and documents public EPUB/PDF download choices when offered. [Open Library, Reading FAQ](https://openlibrary.org/help/faq/reading).
- **Search:** `https://openlibrary.org/search.json?q=...&fields=...&page=1&limit=...` returns work and edition information. Use selected fields and pagination. `lang` influences preferred editions; a language constraint in the query filters results. The optional availability payload is cached/heuristic and explicitly unsuitable as the authoritative lending decision. [Open Library, Search API](https://openlibrary.org/dev/docs/api/search).
- **Details:** `/works/{workId}.json`, `/works/{workId}/editions.json`, `/books/{editionId}.json` and `/isbn/{isbn}.json` provide metadata. The old `/api/books` is now legacy. Its `preview_url` exists even for `preview: noview`, so a URL alone does not prove readability. [Open Library, Books APIs](https://openlibrary.org/dev/docs/api/books).
- **Item files:** `https://archive.org/metadata/{identifier}` returns item metadata and a `files` array including actual names, formats, sizes and checksums. Select a real supported file rather than inventing a filename. Missing/deleted/unavailable items must be handled. [Internet Archive, Metadata Read](https://archive.org/developers/md-read.html).
- **Stable URLs:** the source page is `https://archive.org/details/{identifier}` and an individual file uses `https://archive.org/download/{identifier}/{filename}`. Archive warns against saving the numbered storage-server URL reached after a redirect because items move between servers. [Internet Archive, Items and archival URLs](https://archive.org/developers/items.html).
- **Loans and DRM:** current lending guidance describes one-hour loans through online BookReader, with some fourteen-day loans offering encrypted downloads for LCP-compatible readers. Its page still contains older Adobe wording farther down; the current opening sections explicitly specify LCP. Availability and format vary by item. [Internet Archive, Borrowing](https://help.archive.org/help/borrowing-from-the-lending-library/).
- **API conduct:** Open Library asks for human-triggered requests, caching, identification with application/contact details, batching rather than hundreds of individual lookups, and no HTML scraping or IP distribution. Limits are one request/second unidentified and three identified; bulk metadata belongs in monthly dumps. These APIs are not intended as high-traffic commercial backend infrastructure. [Open Library, API guidelines](https://openlibrary.org/developers/api).
- **Archive automation:** identify tool and version in User-Agent, plus model for AI agents. Honor 429 and Retry-After, bound concurrency, cache and back off on errors. This page was readable through a direct HTTPS fetch after the web retrieval tool timed out. [Internet Archive, Bots and automated access](https://archive.org/developers/bots.html).

## Catalog integration inferences

Use search for discovery and recheck the chosen edition/item when the reader opens its details. Treat Preview, Read externally, Borrow externally and Download as different outcomes. Offer local import only for an actually accessible, unrestricted supported EPUB/PDF with appropriate item rights information; a catalog hit, public metadata, or preview link is insufficient. Do not route loans, print-disability access or encrypted files through the ordinary local importer. Keep provider attribution and the original item page available.

Before shipping, verify the exact item download URL and returned file type, cap file sizes, support cancellation and avoid downloading during search. Revalidate the provider's access policy and actual endpoints in integration tests. Desk research does not establish that every item tagged open has an importable file or that all catalog availability data is current.
